Electrocardiogram


*an electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG) measures

 the electrical signals in the heart

*the P wave is produced when a signal from 

 the SA node spreads through the atria and 

 depolarizes them (.25 mV and .12 s)

*the QRS complex is produced when the signal 

 from the AV node spreads through the

 ventricular myocardium

*the T wave is generated by ventricular 

 repolarization immediately before diastole
