Blood Vessels 

*walls of arteries and veins are composed of 

 three layers called tunics 

*tunica interna (tunica intima) lines the inside 

 of the vessel (exposed to blood) 

*simple squamous epithelium (endothelium) 

 that acts as a selectively permeable barrier 

*tunica media is the middle and usually the 

 thickest layer 

*consists of smooth muscle, collagen and 

 sometimes elastic tissue
